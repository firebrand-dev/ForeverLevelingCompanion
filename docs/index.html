FeaturesFunciones

Everything is included. Nothing is locked.Todo está incluido. Nada está bloqueado.

Step-by-step guideGuía paso a paso

Each step says what to do and with what — “Kill: Mottled Boar”, “Loot the object: Water Pitcher” — with every objective's count. Cada paso dice qué hacer y con qué — “Matar: Jabaly moteado”, “Saquear el objeto: Jarra de agua” — con el conteo de cada objetivo.

Arrow and waypointsFlecha y puntos de ruta

An arrow to the next place, “In the area” when the targets are spread out, and optional TomTom or map waypoints. Una flecha al próximo lugar, “En la zona” cuando los objetivos están repartidos, y puntos de ruta opcionales de TomTom o del mapa.

Quest trackerSeguimiento de misiones

Your quests and your party's progress, with optional announcements in party chat (6 modes, never spammy). Tus misiones y el progreso de tu grupo, con anuncios opcionales en el chat de grupo (6 modos, sin spam).

Help PlayerAyudar a un jugador

Follow a friend's route for a while. Your own guide is saved and you return exactly where you left it. Seguí la ruta de un amigo un rato. Tu guía se guarda y volvés exactamente donde la dejaste.

Level Together and Catch-UpSubir juntos y Ponerse al día

Routes for the whole group, weighted toward whoever is behind. Rutas para todo el grupo, priorizando a quien va atrás.

Dungeon FocusFoco en mazmorras

Dungeons that fit the party's levels and the quests to pick up before going in. Mazmorras acordes al nivel del grupo y las misiones para tomar antes de entrar.

How it worksCómo funciona

FLC picks a guide for your character and shows the next step. Steps complete by themselves when the game confirms them (quest accepted, objectives counted, quest turned in). Use Skip for anything you want to leave out. FLC elige una guía para tu personaje y muestra el próximo paso. Los pasos se completan solos cuando el juego los confirma (misión aceptada, objetivos contados, misión entregada). Usá Skip para saltear lo que no quieras hacer.

In a party, members who also use FLC share quest progress (only after each one agrees with /flc share yes, and only with the group). En grupo, los miembros que también usan FLC comparten el progreso de las misiones (solo si cada uno acepta con /flc share yes, y solo con el grupo).

Changelog

0.8.1 — 2026-11-04

  • First public version. Leveling guides with an arrow, quest tracking and Party Mode (help a player, level together).
  • Guides window (/flc guides): choose the guide to follow. Leveling routes by zone, dungeon and raid guides, only your character's, with their levels and your progress. Automatic gives the choice back to FLC.
  • Quests by themselves: FLC accepts and turns in the guide's quests (never in combat, never with Shift held). On a reward choice FLC never picks: it marks the one that helps you, or the one a vendor pays most for. /flc auto or Options to turn it off.
  • Minimap button: left click shows or hides FLC; right click, a menu with Guides, Loot Atlas, Party Mode, Appearance, Options and Report a bug. Shift + drag moves it.
  • Dungeon (23) and raid (12) guides: the way in, the quest objectives inside, each boss in the game's order, the way out. Boss card with its abilities; what is not confirmed yet carries "(?)".
  • Class and dungeon quest advisors, Smart Loot Advisor and Loot Atlas with a wishlist.
  • Adaptive look: adapts to ElvUI, Tukui, NDui, RealUI, SpartanUI and GW2 UI, or to one of its own skins. Welcome setup on the first start (/flc setup).
  • /flc review and /flc bug make a text to copy and report; /flc version tells you when someone in your group has a newer version.

FAQ

Is it really free?¿Es realmente gratis?

Yes. There is no premium version and nothing is locked. Blizzard's add-on policy requires add-ons to be free, and FLC follows it.Sí. No hay versión premium y nada está bloqueado. La política de addons de Blizzard exige que sean gratuitos, y FLC la cumple.

Does it work without the quest catalog?¿Funciona sin el catálogo de misiones?

Yes, with its built-in sample guides. The catalog pack adds routes for most zones.Sí, con sus guías de ejemplo. El paquete del catálogo agrega rutas para la mayoría de las zonas.

Does it send my data anywhere?¿Envía mis datos a algún lado?

No. Nothing leaves the game. Quest progress is shared only with your party, only after you agree, and only for the quests involved.No. Nada sale del juego. El progreso se comparte solo con tu grupo, solo si aceptás, y solo de las misiones involucradas.

The arrow or a step looks wrong. What do I do?La flecha o un paso parecen estar mal. ¿Qué hago?

Type /flc review on that step, copy the text and report it at FLC-reportes with what the game shows. What is not confirmed yet is marked "in review".Escribí /flc review en ese paso, copiá el texto y reportalo en FLC-reportes con lo que muestra el juego. Lo que todavía no está confirmado dice "en revisión".
